    About MediRoute Healthcare Platform

    Integrated Hospital Decision Support & Emergency Management System

    System Overview

    MediRoute Healthcare Platform is an enterprise emergency dispatch and hospital resource allocation system designed to streamline patient intake, specialist matching, bed management, and consultation scheduling across hospital networks.

    Smart Dispatch & Routing

    Automatically identifies the closest suitable hospital branch based on patient location, specialty requirement, and live bed availability, calculating optimal travel routes and transport options.

    Resource Management

    Real-time tracking and pre-reservation of ICU beds, consultation rooms, emergency equipment, and specialized medical staff prior to patient arrival.

    Doctor Consultation Portal

    Dedicated doctor dashboards allowing consultants to view assigned patient appointments, update medical notes, track room assignments, and manage patient care.

    Role-Based Security

    Multi-tier role-based access control protecting patient privacy and access for Administrators, Operators, Doctors, and Patients.
